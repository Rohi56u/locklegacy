Frequently Asked

How do you handle death certificates? expand_more

We require verified, notarized digital copies which are then authenticated through our partner legal network before any vault unlocking procedure begins.

Can you recover my lost master password? expand_more

No. By design, we employ a zero-knowledge architecture. If you lose your master password and your emergency kit, your data is permanently inaccessible.

Do you support cryptocurrency assets? expand_more

Yes. You can securely store seed phrases and wallet details within encrypted notes, protected by the same military-grade encryption as the rest of your vault.

How do I delete my account and data? expand_more

Account deletion can be initiated from your Security Settings. This action permanently cryptographically shreds your vault and cannot be undone.
