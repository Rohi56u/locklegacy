Frequently Asked Questions

Who owns my data? expand_more
You do. Always. We provide the vault, but you hold the only keys. We cannot access, read, or sell your personal information.
Can I cancel anytime? expand_more
Yes, and you can export all your data before leaving. There are no lock-in contracts or hidden cancellation fees.
How do recovery kits work? expand_more
Recovery kits are local decryption keys that never touch our servers. They allow you to regain access to your vault if you lose your master password, ensuring you're never permanently locked out.
What do my nominees receive? expand_more
A guided, secure dossier only when the continuity protocol initiates. They will not have access to your data beforehand, preserving your privacy during your lifetime.
